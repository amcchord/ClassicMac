THINGS TO TRY IN COPLAND

1. Open Anarcho and write a short observation about the desktop. Save it in My Documents, close it, and reopen it. Restart Copland and check that it is still there.

2. Open GXSlidemaster. Use the arrow keys to compare character forms, positioning, justification and interactive line layout. The demos are live graphics, not screenshots.

3. Deal a game in Eric's Solitaire. Try moving a card into an empty free cell at the bottom. Command-N gives you another board.

4. Play a small MineSweeper board. Double-click opens a cell; a single click marks a suspected mine.

5. Read Interface Field Notes in Reading. Compare the unfinished Finder with the apps on your screen. Record what is consistent and what is missing.

6. Follow the links in Builds and Sources on your host Mac to study Apple's original developer documentation.

Save before quitting. Some classic apps leave the preview in a bad state on exit; restart between activities if the Finder stops responding. Do not judge all historical apps by whether their first window appears: editing, saving and quitting can expose different bugs.
