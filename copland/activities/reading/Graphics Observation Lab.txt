GRAPHICS OBSERVATION LAB
An original ClassicMac activity

Open GXSlidemaster and use the arrow keys to move through the demonstration.

Record the differences between character forms, text positioning, justification and the interactive line-layout examples. Watch the changing samples long enough to distinguish a live effect from a static illustration.

Questions for your notebook:
- Which changes affect individual characters, and which affect an entire line?
- What happens to spacing when the layout changes?
- Which effects would have been useful in a word processor or page-layout program?
- Which parts of the demonstration still feel familiar today?

Quit GXSlidemaster when finished. If another classic app leaves Finder unresponsive on exit, use ClassicMac's Restart before the next activity.
