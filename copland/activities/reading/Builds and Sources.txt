COPLAND BUILDS AND SOURCES
ClassicMac research notes, September 2026

D11E4 is the Copland build on this disk. The original DDK CD contains developer manuals and sample source code; those are useful primary sources when studying the proposed architecture.

The D7E1 archive contains a Scarecrow system, an AppearanceCP app, MacBrowser, and two themes. Its AppearanceCP cannot simply be moved into D11E4: it requires a different FileSystems library. A full D7E1 boot is not qualified in this release.

The archive labeled Copland 8.0.B5 on WinWorld appears to be a different product: its nested contents are a 1997 Mac OS 8 installer with conventional control panels and 68040 support. A more complete shipping-Mac-OS beta does not establish a more complete Copland rewrite.

More installed files do not implement missing OS APIs. Test an app's actual operations, not just its launch screen.
