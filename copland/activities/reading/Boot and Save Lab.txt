BOOT AND SAVE LAB
An original ClassicMac activity

A desktop screenshot proves that a system started. It does not prove that its applications can save work or recover from a restart.

1. Launch Anarcho from Activities.
2. Write three short lines describing the desktop.
3. Save the document in My Documents with a unique name.
4. Close the document, choose File > Open and reopen it.
5. Add another line and save again.
6. Restart Copland through ClassicMac, launch Anarcho and reopen your document.
7. Compare the result with your expectations.

The emulator now keeps guest time across runs. Before that change, a restarted machine could create directory modification dates earlier than their creation dates. Copland rejected those records. This is an example of a virtual machine needing persistent hardware state as well as a disk image.

The clipboard compatibility fix handles zero-byte operations. Ordinary positive-length copy/paste still follows the original implementation. Try copying a line, pasting it and saving the result.

Keep important work on your host Mac. A successful experiment does not make this unfinished OS dependable.
