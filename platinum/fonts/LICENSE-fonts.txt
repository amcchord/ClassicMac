RetroBoot bitmap fonts

  RetroBoot System 12  (src/rb-system-12.glyphs  -> RetroBoot-System-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Regular 12 (src/rb-regular-12.glyphs -> RetroBoot-Regular-12.bdf)
  RetroBoot Small 10   (src/rb-small-10.glyphs   -> RetroBoot-Small-10.bdf)

Provenance: the original fonts were pixel art drawn for RetroBoot in
October 2026. An earlier System 12 ASCII revision used the M5Tab-Macintosh
Charcoal12 recreation (bcfa1649bcbd5a6c433ad4d09b93b543ae768275).

In November 2026 ClassicMac measured the 224 Mac Roman characters and their
advances independently from unsmoothed QuickDraw DrawChar/CharWidth output
in Mac OS 9.2.1: Charcoal 12, Geneva 12 and Geneva 9, respectively. Small 10
retains its existing UI role name but contains the native Geneva 9 bitmap.
Non-Mac-Roman fallback glyphs retain the original RetroBoot artwork.
The reference utility, bitmap measurement tool, source-frame hashes and
independent test atlases are documented in platinum/reference/README.md
and Fixtures/Platinum/native-fonts.json. No original Apple font program was
extracted or included. BDF files are the editable bitmap recreations;
rebuild embedded TrueType with build_platinum_swift.py.
