# ClassicMac 2.1.4

ClassicMac 2.1.4 fixes the ATI/OpenGL triangle-fan topology bug that caused
Oni's Combat Training room to fragment into giant wedges during a later scene
transition. The formerly corrupt +45-second transition now passes its visual
oracle and remains on GXMetal with no rejected geometry or fallback frames.

This is a focused compatibility release, not a claim that every OpenGL or RAVE
game is now supported.

## ATI/OpenGL geometry correction

- Apple's private ATI slots 49/50 are now decoded as untextured/textured
  center-plus-contiguous-rim triangle fans. A four-vertex fan emits
  `(v0,v1,v2)` and `(v0,v2,v3)`.
- Direct-flush fans, where the center and rim pointers initially match, are
  normalized without losing the center. Split-center clipped fans preserve
  their separately supplied center and contiguous rim.
- Slots 51/52 are decoded as untextured/textured triangle strips. Later
  volatile PowerPC registers are ignored instead of being mistaken for
  callback arguments.
- Triangle-list, fan, and strip callbacks accept filled polygon mode and reject
  unsupported point/line modes rather than silently rasterizing them as filled
  triangles.

Mac OS 9's OpenGL implementation still owns OpenGL state, transforms,
lighting, clipping, and API behavior. GXMetal implements the low-level ATI and
QuickDraw 3D RAVE contracts used by Apple's stack; this release does not add a
second OpenGL implementation.

## Diagnostics and automation

- Diagnostic schema 1.0.28 records current and maximum per-frame geometry
  callback counts and preserves the first converted triangle at a generic
  256-callback burst boundary. The decoder remains compatible with the
  supported append-only 1.0.12 through 1.0.27 layouts.
- The shortened Oni pair, AGL/conformance pair, and four public-RAVE game
  routes run from independent clones of one immutable, reboot-verified base.
  Mac OS 9's improper-shutdown disk check is disabled by the preparation
  automation, avoiding an unnecessary wait after disposable VM shutdowns.
- The four-game semantic sweep ran with four concurrent VMs and completed in
  166 seconds wall-clock time.

## Reviewed rc23 qualification evidence

- Oni's coherent +30-second route passed with a pale-corruption fraction of
  `0.000000`.
- Oni's formerly corrupt +45-second route passed at `0.005498`, below the
  `0.10` rejection threshold that rc21 failed at `0.106921`. The trace ends at
  private frame 4,832 with 193,603 method-50 fan calls and zero context
  fallbacks, rejected inputs, rejected triangles, or geometry anomalies.
- GXMetal AGL Probe reported `PASS_ACCELERATED` across all six filled primitive
  modes, texture, blending, depth, clipped texture, readback, presentation,
  resource deletion, and teardown.
- GXMetal Test passed its public RAVE and ATI-private contract and measured
  GXMetal at 50,710 microseconds versus Apple Software RAVE at 524,466
  microseconds, a 10.34-times comparison-workload speedup in this VM run.
- Bugdom, Future Cop: LAPD, Combat Mission, and Weekend Warrior passed their
  reviewed scripted gameplay/input smokes on four concurrent clones.

## Compatibility limits

This fix materially improves confidence in other games using the same ATI fan
and strip paths, but compatibility is not universal. Oni's shortened Combat
Training transition is qualified; longer gameplay, audio, repeated
launch/quit behavior, and complete lifecycle coverage remain open. Points and
lines, rarer vendor state and texture formats, display-mode changes, and
audio-sensitive game startup still need broader real-game testing.
